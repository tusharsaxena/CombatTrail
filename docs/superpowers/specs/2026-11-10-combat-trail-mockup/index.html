Ka0s Combat Trail — every window

Combat Trail replaces CastTrail. It scrolls your casts and your pet's casts as icons along two lanes, climbs your DPS live on a HUD while you fight, and keeps every fight that passes your filters in a Results window built on DPS Race's: a table, a fight detail, an ability timeline, and compare against your best. All figures, names and fights below are made-up samples.

1 · The game screenIn combat: lanes and the live DPS HUD

Two lanes sit under the character, both free-floating by default and moved in Edit Mode. The HUD fades in on the first hit of every combat and fades out a few seconds after it ends.

2 · DPS HUDLive climb, fight over, and the pace bar

Numbers come from Blizzard's damage meter. In combat the game hides those figures from addon math, so the HUD can draw bars and print amounts but never work out percentages. Those arrive on the fight-over card. Pace compares against your best fight on the same boss or target.

    Pet damage is in the totals. Pet spells carry a green paw and a green bar.

    3 · Lane anchoringFree, docked, or on the unit frame

    Each lane picks its anchor on Lanes ▸ Size and position. Free is the default, and Edit Mode shows a blue box to drag. The other two follow their host when it moves.

    4 · Settings ▸ AddOns ▸ Ka0s Combat TrailThe settings panel

    Pick a page on the left. The Lanes page works like Panel Master's: pick Player or Pet from the dropdown, then every tab below edits that lane. There is no "new lane" field. Click the pages, the lane dropdown and the tabs.

    5 · Results windowFight history and fight detail

    Opens from the HUD's chart button, /ct results, a key binding or the minimap menu. It lists every stored fight, newest first, grouped by encounter or target and difficulty. Click a row to see its detail.

    6 · Ability timelineEvery cast, this fight against your best

    Opens from the detail's Timeline button, over the Results window at its size. Each ability has two lanes: this fight in gold, the compared fight in blue. Long cooldowns sort to the top. Ctrl+wheel zooms and Shift+wheel pans.

    7 · CompareSide by side with your best, or any fight you pick

    Picking the other fight

    • Compare vs best uses your best on the same encounter and difficulty, or the runner-up if this fight is the best.
    • Right-click any row and pick Compare With This Fight. The pick lasts until you clear it or reload.
    • The gear slots that changed and a Different loadout mark sit under the spells.

    8 · Gear viewWhat you wore and the stats you pulled with

    Snapshot at the pull

    • Gear, stats and the talent loadout are read when combat starts, and again out of combat if anything came back hidden.
    • A tinted slot changed since the compared fight. Item level and stat deltas are green when up and red when down.
    • Hover an item for its tooltip, and shift-click it to link it.

    9 · ChatSlash commands and the shared summary

    Same as every Ka0s addon

    • Every setting is also a slash path, for example /ct set lanes.pet.width 360.
    • The debug console, diagnostics, test mode and sim mode work as they do in DPS Race.
    • Test mode fills the lanes, the HUD and the Results window with sample data, behind a strip saying so.
