## The title-bar pass (rulings 3 and 4)

Every title-bar control this addon draws goes through two places: `Style.ControlButton` with
`Style.AddToStrip` (the marks) and `NS.MakeCloseButton` (the close, Core's `MakeCloseButton` with
the owner). The library's close already does what the rulings ask (`Close` through
`AttachTitleTip`, `close.rest` following `icon.tint`, `close.hot` under the pointer), so the change
is in `modules/Style.lua` only. `W.HeaderButtons` was not adopted: the two seams were the smaller
change.

- **Tooltip.** `Style.ControlButton` hooks `LibKa0s-Tooltip-1.0`'s `AttachTitleTip` with the owner
  (`b.tip`, read on each hover, in `gtip.title`, placed above by `PlaceAttached`, flipped below only
  without room, hidden on leave). Before, it was the client's `ANCHOR_TOP` in `gtip.value`. A
  degraded load (no Tooltip library) keeps `ANCHOR_TOP`, now in `gtip.title`.
- **Colors.** Rest `icon.tint` (`Style.REST`, unchanged). Hover is now `text.link` (`Style.HOT`, new),
  where it was the header gold (`text.accent`). These controls have no glow texture, so the hover is
  the tint alone, as on the library's header marks. The close keeps the library's `close.hot`. The
  lock carries no state color: its state is its art (lock or unlock), so it rests and lights like
  the other marks.
- **The timeline's zoom strip** (the `?`, scroll, reset and zoom marks on the Casts heading line) is
  inside its window, not on a title bar. It keeps the header gold under the pointer
  (`Style.NewStrip(Style.GOLD)`), and its tooltips now open above it through the same attached
  placement.

| Window | Title-bar controls | Tooltip | Rest / hover |
|---|---|---|---|
| DPS HUD | Results, Race (Retail, Race mode on), Settings, Lock, Close | each its words; Close: "Close" | `icon.tint` / `text.link`; Close `icon.tint` / `close.hot` |
| Race window (Retail) | Attach, Lock, Settings, Close | each its words; Close: "Close" | as above |
| Results | View toggle (fold/unfold, words follow the view), Settings, Close | each its words; Close: "Close" | as above |
| Compare | Close | "Close" | `icon.tint` / `close.hot` |
| Gear view | Close | "Close" | `icon.tint` / `close.hot` |
| Ability timeline | Close | "Close" | `icon.tint` / `close.hot` |

Tests: `tests/test_title_bar.lua` (new, 7 cases, red against the old `ControlButton` first: the
tooltip was owned at `ANCHOR_TOP`). One case per window fires every control's enter and leave, under
Ka0s Default and under the first other skin whose `icon.tint`, `text.link`, `close.hot` and
`gtip.title` all differ from Ka0s Default's (Azeroth at this tag; Obsidian shares the gold
`gtip.title`), and a guard case holds that such a skin exists. `tests/test_hud_race_button.lua`'s
hover case is re-pinned from the gold to `Style.HOT` under ruling 4.
