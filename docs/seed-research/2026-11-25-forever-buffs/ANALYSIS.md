## Judgment calls applied

- **A stat drink counts as buff food.** #13 says to key a stat drink on its buff. The rums and
  Raptor Punch carry a stat aura (29), so they are accepted. Their drunkenness is not what the Used
  column lists.
- **Rule 2 is one trigger hop, plus the use spell itself.** That is enough for every food the
  review named. A food whose buff sits two hops away would come in through a deeper rule, ruled the
  same way.
- **Dummy-aura and unknown-source ids** follow CL-13a's calls: an aura named after a known world
  buff is accepted, and an unknown source is rejected for review.
